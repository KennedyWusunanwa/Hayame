# Hayame Company Constitution Check

Date checked: 2026-07-06

## Conclusion

No Hayame company constitution was found in this workspace or on the public Hayame website.

If Hayame has a company constitution, it is likely stored outside this product/code repository, such as with incorporation records, legal counsel, company secretary records, or the relevant company registrar filings.

## Evidence Summary

- No file names in the workspace matched constitution, articles, memorandum, bylaws, incorporation, registration, shareholder, director, or governance document patterns.
- No text matches were found for exact corporate-document terms such as "company constitution", "articles of association", "memorandum of association", "certificate of incorporation", or "shareholder agreement".
- Public Hayame pages expose customer-facing legal/support content only.
- The Protection page states that protection details are informational/placeholders and should not be treated as active insurance. This is unrelated to a company constitution.

## Practical Next Steps

To confirm whether Hayame legally has a company constitution, check outside this codebase:

1. Company incorporation folder or registrar documents.
2. Company secretary records.
3. Legal counsel files.
4. Certificate of Incorporation package.
5. Any filings or extracts from the Office of the Registrar of Companies.

If a constitution exists, store a copy in a controlled legal/admin records location rather than inside the public app repository unless there is a deliberate reason to publish it.
